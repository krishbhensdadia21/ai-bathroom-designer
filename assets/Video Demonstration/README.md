## 📋 Demonstration Highlights

1. **Interactive 3D WebGL Bathroom Studio**:
   - 360° 3D view, **2D architectural floor plan**, and **first-person Walk-In mode**.
   - Real-time **Grid Snap, Wall Snap, and Clearance Zones** for precise product placement.
   - Daylight, Dusk, and Night ambience with **2700K / 4000K / 5000K lighting controls**.
   - Luxury surface styling and dynamically configurable bathroom dimensions.

2. **Official KOHLER Product Catalog & Spatial Planner**:
   - Live catalog covering **Toilets, Vanities, Faucets, Showers, Bathtubs, and Mirrors** with product-level details.
   - Manual placement, rotation, duplication, and removal of KOHLER fixtures directly inside the 3D bathroom.
   - Product cards display **SKU, dimensions, pricing, availability, and fixture specifications**.
   - Supports spatial planning around real KOHLER products rather than generic placeholder models.

3. **Dual-Pathway Generative AI Design Assistant**:
   - **Predefined Constraints Mode** for dimensions, budget, aesthetic theme, and fixture inclusions.
   - **Natural-Language Wishlist Mode** for custom prompts such as room size, users, materials, vanity requirements, and shower preferences.
   - AI automatically **parses custom requirements**, adapts room dimensions, applies budget constraints, and generates a personalized KOHLER suite.
   - Multi-objective **AI Pareto recommendation engine** balances design requirements, product selection, budget utilization, and spatial constraints.

4. **Spatial Intelligence, Compliance & Trade Specification**:
   - Live **NKBA compliance indicator** with clearance validation and conflict alerts.
   - **Wet-Wall alignment engine** for optimized plumbing-stack placement, with demonstrated alignment scoring up to **96%**.
   - Automated **KOHLER Design & Specification Quote** with selected products, quantities, SKUs, dimensions, rough-in details, P-trap and electrical requirements.
   - **Copy Spec, Print Sheet, and Export RFQ JSON** workflows, alongside environmental insights including **WaterSense eligibility and annual potable-water/carbon savings**.
