Case Study Challenge: KOHLER AI Bathroom Designer & Planner

Candidate Solution: Track 1 Case Study

Developed as an individual submission for the KOHLER-MITWPU AI Research Lab Program selection process. Tackling generative design automation, physical constraint optimization, and modern connected smart living.

Track 1 Objective & Expected Outcome

Objective: Build an interactive AI design assistant that takes a customer's constraints (dimensions, budget limits, aesthetic themes, and device catalog specifications) and automates personalized product bundle recommendations.

Expected Outcome: An intelligent recommendation engine outputting optimized product combinations (smart toilets, vanities, thermostatic showers, mirrors, bathtubs) fitting exact physical space and budget parameters, accompanied by an interactive 3D WebGL and 2D CAD representation.

Evaluation Criteria Alignment (100% Weightage)

Approach & Innovation 45% Weight

Dual-mode AI assistant (Predefined constraints & natural-language prompt), NLP dimension & budget extraction, Meta Llama Prompt Guard 22M security, and Multi-Objective optimization with explainable AI cards.

Technical Execution 25% Weight

Three.js WebGL rendering, real-time procedural PBR materials, dynamic architectural rebuilding on dimension change, zero-pricing discrepancy sync, and Vercel edge deployment.

User Experience & Feasibility 20% Weight

Clean default empty room, 1-click Clear Room with dimension reset, manual generate trigger, unified quote BOM with multi-currency (INR, USD, CAD), and Studio Kohler specifier table.

Business & Sustainability 10% Weight

Kohler WaterSense annual water savings calculation, carbon offset tracking, and plumbing wet-wall alignment optimization to reduce installation piping costs.
